# Wetin Be Dis — Cash Prize Launch Pack

Status: **locked launch plan**. Cash mode remains disabled until every go/no-go item is approved.

## Locked launch configuration

- Season: five consecutive weekly rounds.
- Season dates: November 11–December 16, 2026.
- Total prize budget: **₦500,000**.
- Weekly prize budget: **₦100,000**.
- Weekly winners: top three.
- Weekly prizes: **1st ₦50,000, 2nd ₦30,000, 3rd ₦20,000**.
- Participants: no advertised numerical cap, subject to tested platform capacity.
- Attempts: one verified attempt per participant per round.
- Entry model: free-to-enter skill competition; no paid retries or paid advantages.
- Payout method: manual OPay transfers from the operator's account; no OPay integration or partnership assumed.
- Winner handling: identity and payout-account verification, fraud review, then manual payout.
- Ranking: public top-100 standings using player-selected public handles; each authenticated player can see their exact rank after submitting.
- Public ranking data: rank, public handle, score, timing metrics, and provisional/confirmed status only. Never publish legal names, phone numbers, bank details, or verification records.
- Cash mode: remotely controlled and fail-closed until launch approval.

## Recommended pilot

- Free-to-enter skill competition.
- Season prize pool: **₦500,000 total across five weekly rounds**.
- Weekly prize pool: **₦100,000 per round**.
- Weekly prize distribution: **1st ₦50,000, 2nd ₦30,000, 3rd ₦20,000**; this remains changeable until the official rules are approved.
- No participant cap in the rules, subject to platform capacity, abuse controls, and the event closing time.
- Season format: **five consecutive seven-day rounds** from the published opening timestamp to the final closing timestamp.
- Season dates: **Wednesday, November 11 through Wednesday, December 16, 2026**, with each round opening and closing at the same published time in WAT.
- Nigeria-first, limited to locations approved by gaming counsel and the relevant regulator.
- One verified attempt per player per event.
- No paid retries, paid boosts, entry fees, or advantages purchased with money.
- Fixed prize pool announced before the event opens.
- One small closed pilot before any public launch.
- Manual winner review before a payout is released.

“Unlimited participants” means there is no advertised numerical cap. It does not mean the season stays open indefinitely or that a player may submit multiple attempts in a round. Each round needs a fixed closing time, one verified account per person, rate limits, and a tested capacity ceiling.

This is a product recommendation, not a legal conclusion. A Nigerian gaming lawyer must classify the exact contest and confirm the required federal, state, or partner licensing. Nigerian lottery guidance says anyone conducting a lottery must obtain a licence or permit, and NLRC technical requirements call for documented game rules, platform controls, fair-play commitments, and software testing.

## OPay payout position

OPay should be the primary Nigerian payout rail for the pilot, using a verified business or merchant account rather than a personal wallet:

1. Complete OPay business onboarding and confirm the account's transaction limits.
2. Use the operator's account only for manual transfers and follow the account's transaction limits and applicable terms.
3. Do not describe OPay as a sponsor, partner, or product integration without a signed agreement.
4. Never collect a winner's bank details in public chat or in the app's analytics.
5. Verify the winner's legal name against the payout account before sending.
6. Record the transfer reference, amount, currency, timestamp, operator, and delivery status.
7. Have a second-person approval for every payout.
8. Keep a fallback plan for failed, reversed, delayed, or misdirected transfers.

OPay is listed by the Central Bank of Nigeria under the mobile-money-operator category, and OPay advertises business accounts and merchant transfers. This plan does not represent OPay as a sponsor or partner.

## Required documents

### 1. Legal and regulatory file

- Contest classification memo.
- Jurisdiction and geo-restriction map.
- Required licence, permit, or licensed-partner confirmation.
- Corporate registration and tax details.
- Counsel-approved official rules.
- Winner terms, privacy notice, and dispute process.

### 2. Official rules must state

- Sponsor/operator identity.
- Eligibility, age, and location restrictions.
- Opening and closing times in a named time zone.
- How to enter and whether entry is free.
- Number of attempts allowed.
- Question, scoring, tie-break, and disqualification rules.
- Prize amounts, currency, and number of winners.
- Winner-selection and verification process.
- Payout timing and failed-payment process.
- Taxes or withholding responsibility.
- Appeals, complaints, and final decision process.
- Responsible-play and account-closure information.
- A clear statement that Apple and Google are not sponsors where required by store rules.

### 3. Finance and payout file

- Dedicated prize budget funded before launch.
- Event-level ledger for prize liability, payouts, fees, and reversals.
- Manual OPay payout procedure and account-limit record.
- Maximum prize and maximum daily payout limits.
- Bank-detail verification procedure.
- Two-person payout approval.
- Reconciliation report after every event.
- Accounting and tax review.

### 4. Player protection and verification

- Age gate and location check.
- Identity verification before payout.
- One-person/one-account controls.
- Duplicate-account and device-risk detection.
- Sanctions and fraud screening through an appropriate provider.
- Self-exclusion and account suspension workflow.
- Data minimisation, retention, deletion, and breach procedures.

Nigeria's Data Protection Commission identifies lawful, transparent processing and data-subject rights as core obligations under the Nigeria Data Protection Act. Identity and payout data must therefore have a documented purpose, access control, retention period, and deletion process.

### 5. Fair-play and security file

- Server-authoritative event creation, scoring, and finalisation.
- Immutable event and result records.
- Signed question-pack/version identifiers.
- Replay and answer-timing anomaly detection.
- Admin audit logs and least-privilege access.
- Rate limiting, bot detection, and device integrity checks.
- Incident-response runbook.
- Independent review of the first pilot's results.

### Public ranking policy

The leaderboard is intentionally public so participants can follow the competition and see where they stand. Public output must use a stable, player-selected handle and must not expose identity or payout information. A player can request correction or removal of their public handle through support, subject to the active competition, review, dispute, and accounting retention windows.

## Operating procedure for one event

1. Fund and approve the ₦100,000 round prize pool.
2. Publish the event rules and prize amount.
3. Open the event only in approved jurisdictions.
4. Accept verified players and issue one server-controlled attempt.
5. Freeze entries at the deadline.
6. Finalise scores and run fraud checks.
7. Publish winner handles or anonymised references, not sensitive identity data.
8. Contact winners through the verified account channel.
9. Complete identity and OPay payout-account checks.
10. Obtain payout approval and send through the approved method.
11. Record delivery evidence and reconcile the ledger.
12. Handle appeals, then close the event with an audit report.

## Store and release gates

Apple and Google require licensing or permissions where applicable, geographic controls, age safeguards, and appropriate contest/gambling disclosures. The mobile apps must keep cash disabled unless the release has:

- approved rules URL;
- approved privacy and support URLs;
- jurisdiction configuration;
- age and location enforcement;
- payout and verification workflows;
- completed security review;
- store-policy review;
- regulator/counsel sign-off;
- tested emergency disable and rollback.

## Go/no-go checklist

Go only when all answers are yes:

- Has counsel classified the contest?
- Has the required licence or licensed partner been confirmed?
- Is the manual OPay payout procedure documented and within the account's limits?
- Is the complete prize pool already reserved?
- Are official rules published and reviewed?
- Are age, identity, jurisdiction, and fraud checks working?
- Can the team pause the event and revoke cash mode remotely?
- Can every payout be reconciled to a verified winner and transfer reference?
- Have Apple and Google submission requirements been satisfied?
- Has a closed pilot completed without unresolved payout or integrity incidents?
